Gmail

  1. Open Settings > See all settings > General, scroll to Signature.
  2. Create new (or click your existing one), click in the box and paste.
  3. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Apple Mail

  1. Mail > Settings > Signatures, click + to add one.
  2. Untick "Always match my default message font", then paste into the right-hand box.
  3. Pick it as the default for your account.
